# Do the glyph metrics point at real pixels?

The parsed metrics say where each glyph lives; this crops every rectangle out of the
real atlas and counts ink, so a metric that points at empty space shows up as a
finding instead of a blank label. Generated by `tools/verify_font_glyph_content.py`.

## Counts (current run)

| font | glyphs | with ink | empty | max ink fraction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 04b03_16.fnt | 100 | 53 | **47** | 1.0 |
| 04b03black_16.fnt | 100 | 99 | 1 | 0.543 |
| 04b03embossed_16.fnt | 100 | 99 | 1 | 0.9145 |
| Blockheads_64.fnt | 100 | 99 | 1 | 0.9059 |
| Blockheadsblack_64.fnt | 100 | 99 | 1 | 0.9059 |

Totals: 5 fonts, 5 atlases present, 500 glyphs, **449 with ink, 51 empty**.

## The 51 empties are two different things

- **47 of them are `04b03_16.fnt`** - the font whose descriptor declares a 256x128
  canvas while the shipped PNG is 2048x1024 (`FONT_ASSET_TABLES.md`). Cropping at the
  declared coordinates samples the top-left eighth.
- **1 per font is the space character** (`char_id = 32`): `04b03black_16`,
  `04b03embossed_16` and both 64px faces have exactly that one inkless glyph (its
  rectangle is zero-width), which is what a space looks like in a bitmap atlas.
  `04b03_16`'s 47 empties start with the same `char_id = 32` and continue with real
  glyphs (`33`, `35`, `36`, `37`, `38`, `40`, `41`, ...) - the scale effect below.

## The scale hypothesis, confirmed by pixels

`04b03_16.fnt` is also the font with `atlas_size / scale_w = 8`, so the tool re-crops
its rectangles at that ratio:

| sampling | glyphs with ink |
|---|---:|
| declared canvas (256x128) | 53 / 100 |
| implied scale (x8) | **99 / 100** |

The 47 empty rectangles fill in at 8x, leaving only the space glyph. That turns the
earlier reading ("the metrics are internally consistent, so the sampler owes a
scale") into a measured result: the metrics are right, the atlas is 8x, and anything
drawing this face at declared coordinates renders mostly nothing.

## Boundaries

- "Ink" = any non-zero alpha in the cropped rectangle. A glyph whose art is purely
  additive with zero alpha would read as empty even though it draws.
- The check proves the rectangles land on art; it says nothing about glyph order,
  kerning or advance behaviour.
- No device run and no text was rendered.

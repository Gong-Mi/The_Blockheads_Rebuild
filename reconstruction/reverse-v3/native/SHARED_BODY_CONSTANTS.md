# What the shared body's six constants are

R22 decoded six constants compared against `[fp,-0x540]` in the tile drawing shared
body; R27 showed all six are members of the direct-case image set. This closes the
question by joining them back to the contents that produce them - and by checking who
writes the compared slot. Generated by `tools/join_shared_body_constants.py`.

Every constant is produced by **exactly one** content, and the six are the
leaf/trunk-leaf pairs of three tree species: **Apple, Cherry, Maple**.

## Why the comparison means something

The compared slot is not a mystery value: **all 61 direct cases write
`[fp,-0x540]`/`[fp,-0x544]`** in their own body (scanned 40 instructions from each
case target). So the slot carries the draw image the case just chose, and the shared
body is asking "is the tile I am drawing one of these six leaf images?" before
overriding the draw pair and the mode.

## What is not claimed

- The six are **not "all leaves"**: 31 contents are leaf-named, and only these six are
  special-cased. Why these three species - the seasonal-colour overlay is a plausible
  reason, but nothing here tests it.
- The candidate names are the in-repo C+ correlation labels, not extracted strings.
- No runtime trace, no frame capture: the analysis is static, on instruction streams
  and join keys.

## Boundaries

- The join is numeric: `draw_image` for a content equals the constant. That is exactly
  the relation the code compares, but it does not prove the comparison fires at runtime.
- The write-site scan covers the first 40 instructions of each case body.

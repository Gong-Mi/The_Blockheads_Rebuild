- **What the class is**: the freight truck of the electric railway = a TrainCar (E99 family) subclass that carries a child **Chest** on its platform (ivars `platformCube@208` / `chestCube@212` / `poleCube@216` / `chest@220` / `needsChestSave@224`; super TrainCar adds the wheel pair @96/@104, the consist links @168/172/176, shader@56 + worldObjectShader@60 + tile/tileDestruct/item textures @64/68/72, visualVelocity@128, rotationAnimationTimer@92, engineIsRight@180).

# Tile-record fields measured on real save data

The static side says which offsets the drawing path reads; this says what a real world
actually stores there. Generated by `tools/histogram_save_tile_fields.py` from the
offline server-world archive (40 `blocks` records, one gzip blob per chunk).

## Counts (current run)

| element | value |
|---|---:|
| blocks records | 40 |
| tiles sampled | 40,960 |
| decompressed bytes per record | 65,541 |
| tiles per record | 1,024 (= 65,536/64, plus the 5 trailing PhysicalBlock bytes) |

## What the data settles

- `raw[0]` and `raw[1]` are always non-zero and take small domains - tile type and
  back-wall type, as the header names them - so the sample is real terrain, not padding.
- `raw[3]` (`contentsType`) is zero in 98.6% of tiles and takes 19 values when set,
  matching "contents present or not" rather than a continuous field.
- **`raw[5]` and `raw[12]` are zero in every one of the 40,960 tiles.** The variant
  selector read by all nine direct case bodies and the field the tail compares against
  `0x45` are simply unused in this world: whatever sets them, this save never does. That
  removes the "`raw[12]` holds a content value" reading for this sample - a content value
  would show up as non-zero on tiles that carry contents - and it means the tail's
  override branch never fires here.
- `raw[8]` is non-zero in 58 tiles, and those tiles are a single state:

  | type | back wall | contents | raw[7] | tiles |
  |---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | 2 | 2 | 6 | 255 | 26 |
  | 2 | 2 | 3 | 255 | 11 |
  | 2 | 2 | 8 | 255 | 7 |
  | 2 | 2 | 0 | 255 | 4 |
  | 2 | 2 | 18 | 255 | 4 |

  Contents 3, 5, 6, 8 and 18 are leaf/trunk-leaf contents from the catalog, and raw[7] is
  255 (`temperatureScaleByte`) on all of them, with raw[8] landing in 124-133 - i.e. the
  neighbourhood of 127, which is exactly the centre the renderer subtracts
  (`(raw[8] - 127) * constant`). Read together: these are cold-region tiles carrying
  plants, and the draw is tinted by that byte. The tinting reading is an inference from
  the arithmetic plus this co-occurrence, not an observed render.

## Boundaries

- **One world.** 40 chunks of a single save; another world may exercise `raw[5]` and
  `raw[12]`. Their emptiness is a fact about this data, not about the format.
- The `int16` windows (`raw[5..6]`, `[6..7]`, `[7..8]`, `[8..9]`) are recorded in the JSON
  because `temperatureOffset()` is an `int16` in the header and the window is not pinned;
  `[8..9]` has the most small non-zero values (20 kinds within +/-2000).
- Value domains only: nothing here says how the renderer uses them beyond the arithmetic
  recovered from the code.

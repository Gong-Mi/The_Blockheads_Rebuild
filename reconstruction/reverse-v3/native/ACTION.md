- **Accessor taxonomy (the whole class is a property/member surface)**: 16 getters + 16 setters, one pair per ivar, plus `.cxx_construct`, `dealloc` and the big initializer. Five accessor dialects, all keyed by the ivar offset cell:
  - object ivars (interactionItem@16, craftableItemObject@40, blockCube@48, inventoryChange@64): getters tail-branch to `objc_getProperty(self,_cmd,offset,1)` (ac_02/05/11/16); setters `bl objc_setProperty_atomic(self,_cmd,value,offset)` (ac_20/23/27/32) - the pair is consistent with atomic property semantics.
  - char ivars (complete@5, inProgress@4, isAI@6): getters `ldrsb` (ac_03/09/17), setters `strb` inside a `dmb ish` pair (ac_21/26/33); no dmb on the read side.
  - short ivars (craftCountOrExtraData@44, interactionItemIndex@20, interactionItemSubIndex@22): getters `ldrsh` (ac_04/12/13), setters `strh` in a dmb pair (ac_22/28/29).

- **ac_10 the 12-argument initializer** (205 words, the batch heavyweight; 4 calls / 2 branches): builds `struct objc_super {self, Action class}` on the stack (class via superref 0x00e8bd04 -> 0x00e90da0, matching class_metadata class_addr) and calls `objc_msgSendSuper2(&super, @selector(init))`; a `cmp/bne` guard returns nil when `[super init]` came back nil, then the 11 params are stored into the 11 ivars. Three object params are stored only after `objc_msgSend(param, @selector(retain))` (interactionItem, craftableItemObject, inventoryChange; selref 'retain' @0x00e808ec); the other eight are plain stores (goalTilePos pair, goalInteraction, pathType, itemIndex/itemSubIndex as strh, interactionObjectID as an 8-byte pair written with `str r0,[r2,ip]!; str r1,[r2,4]`, craftCountOrExtraData as strh, isAI as strb). ## Boundaries

- **ac_33 (setIsAI:) is TRIMMED by construction**: the roster bound cuts the body at the next IMP (0x736e14 `Action -[animationTimer]`) while this IMP's ARM.exidx entry extends to 0x736ea8. The captured 17-word stream is the complete standard byte-setter (dmb ish / strb / dmb ish + epilogue + 2 pool words) - recorded as-is, not extended into the neighbour, so any bytes belonging to the following methods are deliberately absent.
- **ac_01/ac_33 share an exidx bound**: the exidx entry covering 0x736e14..0x736ea8 spans animationTimer, setAnimationTimer: and the tail of setIsAI:'s range; per-body bounds therefore used the next-IMP rule, and both bodies end with their own epilogues (`bx lr`), so no instruction was lost. The pool words of ac_01 sit at 0x736e54/0x736e58, inside the exidx range but before the next IMP.
- **ac_10 frame-slot mapping is a decode, not an encoding read**: the type encoding (`@60@0:4{?=ii}8i16i20@24s28s32Q36@44s48c52@56`) labels the Q at 36 and the tail params at 44/48/52/56, but the instruction stream reads Q at frames 40/44 and tail params at frames 48/52/56/60. The 4-byte shift is explained by 8-byte alignment before the Q (frame 36 padded); the evidence is the actual `ldr rX,[fp,#0x20..0x34]` sequence, and the ivar stores were verified against the store cells, not against the label order. Do not quote the encoder offsets as the callee layout without this note.
